Snow Rush – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Snow Rush ("the game") is an offline mobile snowboarding game. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, unlocked riders, boards and mountains, best distances, missions and settings) is saved only in the game's private storage on your device. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Permissions

The game requests only the vibration permission, used for haptic feedback during play. You can turn vibration off in the game's settings.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling personal data (for example through ads or purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
